Privacy & booking policies

Please review these policies before making an enquiry. They should be checked and finalized for your actual business practices before the website is published.

1. Privacy notice

Information collected: A Day Out may collect your name, contact number, requested service, preferred date and time, venue or city, group size and booking preferences. Please do not send passwords, financial account credentials, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information through the enquiry form.

How information is used: Information is used to answer enquiries, discuss availability, arrange bookings, communicate changes, manage safety and maintain necessary business records.

Sharing: We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with other people for publicity without permission, except where sharing is necessary to arrange a booking with your agreement or is required by law.

WhatsApp: If you continue to WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp/Meta and is subject to their own terms and privacy practices. Avoid sharing information that is not necessary for your booking.

Retention and security: Booking information should be accessible only to people who need it, protected against unauthorized access and deleted or anonymised when no longer reasonably necessary, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements. The business should set a specific retention period before publication.

Your choices: You may contact the business to request access to or correction of your personal information and to raise a privacy concern. Rights and obligations depend on applicable law.

Contact: For privacy questions, contact A Day Out through the official WhatsApp number +233 50 459 8324.

2. Booking confirmation, payment and cancellations
  • A booking enquiry is not a confirmed reservation. Confirmation occurs only after the date, time, duration, venue, service, total price and expectations have been agreed in writing.
  • Any deposit, payment deadline, cancellation charge, refund arrangement, lateness policy or extra-time charge must be explained and agreed before payment or confirmation.
  • Transportation within Ashanti Region is included in the listed prices. Extra travel charges may apply elsewhere in Ghana and must be agreed beforehand.
  • International bookings are available on special request and require advance approval. Travel, accommodation where necessary, transport, service fees and additional expenses must be agreed before confirmation.
  • Tickets, meals, accommodation and other third-party costs are separate unless expressly included in the written agreement.
3. Safety and meeting locations
  • No midnight meetings. Half-night bookings must end before midnight.
  • No isolated bush areas, abandoned buildings or locations considered unsafe.
  • The venue, start and end times, transport arrangements and activity plan must be agreed in advance.
  • Either party may decline a venue or end an interaction if they feel unsafe or uncomfortable.
  • For personal safety, consider meeting in a public venue, arranging independent transport and telling a trusted person your plans.
4. Conduct, boundaries and consent

All interactions must be respectful, voluntary and based on clear communication. Companionship does not imply consent to sexual activity or any unwanted physical contact. Either party may set boundaries or withdraw consent at any time. Harassment, threats, coercion, violence, unlawful activity and unwanted touching are not accepted.

Photos, audio recordings or videos must not be taken or shared without the consent of the people involved and in accordance with applicable law.

5. International bookings

Bookings outside Ghana are considered individually and are subject to advance discussion, feasibility, applicable travel and immigration requirements, and approval. The client and A Day Out must agree on service fees, travel, accommodation where necessary, local transport, dates, responsibilities and additional expenses before the booking is confirmed. No international booking is guaranteed until confirmed in writing.

6. Policy updates and legal compliance

A Day Out may update its policies when necessary. Material terms should be communicated before a booking is confirmed. Before publishing, the business owner should review these policies for compliance with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), any applicable Data Protection Commission requirements and the laws of other countries where services are offered. This template is general information, not legal advice.
